Put it in order! 🧩

Each round has a simple everyday task. The steps are all mixed up, and one of them is a silly step that doesn't belong. Build the algorithm by putting only the right steps in the right order, then press Run to see what happens.

An algorithm is a list of clear steps, in the right order, that solves a problem.

Mixed-up steps

Tap a step to add it to your algorithm. Careful: one step doesn't belong!

Your algorithm

Use ↑ ↓ to move a step and ✕ to remove it.

    What happens

    Right order

    Boil the water before adding tea. Steps that depend on each other must come in order.

    No missing steps

    Skip one step and the task fails, even if everything else is perfect.

    No extra steps

    A good algorithm has only the steps that help reach the goal.

    Sometimes order is flexible

    Milk and sugar or tea powder first? Some steps can swap, and it still works.

    How to run this in 5 minutes (for the teacher)
    1. Share your screen and read the purple line about what an algorithm is (30 seconds).
    2. Round 1, Chai: ask students in the chat "What's the first step?", then the next, and so on. Add steps as they say them and press Run (1.5 minutes).
    3. Round 2, ATM: let one student dictate the full order. If it fails, ask the class which step is in the wrong place (1.5 minutes).
    4. Round 3, Plant a seed: quick round. Ask "Which step doesn't belong?" before starting (1 minute).
    5. Finish with the four coloured cards and ask: "What's an algorithm you follow every morning?" (30 seconds).
